Architectural Finishes

Artisanal Texture Collections

Unlike flat paint, our hand-applied mineral textures interact dynamically with light to give rooms tactile depth.

Lime & Clay Blend

Roman Clay Plaster

Hand-applied using a spatula to create a matte, cloud-like plaster look. Formulated from natural ingredients, it offers a soft finish that keeps walls breathable.

Ideal for: Bedrooms, Studies, Living Rooms | Durability: High Breathability
Luxury Metallic Overlay

Burnished Metallic Stucco

An overlay of slaked lime and fine marble dust burnished to a high gloss with highlights of gold, silver, or bronze veining. Catches ambient light to create a rich focus wall.

Ideal for: Foyer Entrances, Fireplace Surrounds | Durability: Water Resistant
Slaked Lime & Minerals

Chalky Lime Wash

Dating back to antiquity, lime wash penetrates masonry or drywall to create a textured, chalky velvet patina that is mold-resistant, odorless, and completely organic.

Ideal for: Brick Facades, Dining Rooms | Durability: Mold & Mildew Resistant

Our Workflow

The Meticulous Finishing Process

We follow a strict multi-phase application protocol to ensure structural bonding and flawless texture reflection.

01

Preparation & Drywall Restoration

We patch structural cracks, joint-tape panels, and sand down imperfections with dustless extractors to achieve a Level 5 smooth canvas.

02

Substrate Mineral Priming

We apply quartz or silicate primers that create a micro-textured bond, allowing subsequent lime and cement plaster layers to fuse chemically.

03

Multi-Layer Hand Troweling

Our artisans apply slaked lime plaster or microcement overlay in multiple micro-thin layers, hand-modulating texture and depth variables.

04

Burnishing & Wax Sealants

For Venetians, we hand-polish with steel spatulas to a glass sheen. Finally, we seal with organic olive oil soap or natural beeswax to resist staining.
