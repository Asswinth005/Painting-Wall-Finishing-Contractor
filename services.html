Our Process

How We Work

A transparent, step-by-step approach that ensures your project is executed with precision and care.

1

Consultation & Assessment

We discuss your vision, assess the space, and provide expert recommendations on finishes, colors, and materials.

2

Color & Material Selection

Using our color lab and material library, we help you select the perfect palette and finish type for your project.

3

Surface Preparation

Meticulous preparation including cleaning, sanding, priming, and repairing any imperfections for a flawless base.

4

Expert Application

Our skilled artisans apply your chosen finish with precision, using time-honored techniques and premium materials.

5

Quality Inspection

We thoroughly inspect every surface, making any necessary touch-ups to ensure perfection before completion.

6

Final Reveal

We walk you through the finished project, provide care instructions, and ensure your complete satisfaction.
