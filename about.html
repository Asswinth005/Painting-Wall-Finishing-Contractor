About Us

Masters of Surface
Artistry

For over a decade, we've been transforming spaces with meticulous craftsmanship, premium materials, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Our Story

From Passion to
Profession

Founded in 2012 by master craftsman James Thornton, Brush & Roll began as a small team of dedicated artisans with a shared vision: to bring unparalleled quality and artistry to every wall, ceiling, and surface.

Today, we've grown into a trusted name in premium wall finishing, serving residential and commercial clients across the region. Our commitment to excellence remains unchanged — every project receives the same meticulous attention to detail that defined our very first commission.
